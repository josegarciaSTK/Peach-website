How we work

Listen, shape, make, tend.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We walk through your business the way a customer would, and write down what’s actually going wrong.

  2. 02

    Shape

    You get a short written scope: what’s included, what isn’t, what we need from you and how we’ll check it works.

  3. 03

    Make

    We design and build in small steps you can see. Nothing goes live without your approval.

  4. 04

    Tend

    We test it, hand it over with notes, and look after it afterwards if you’d like.

Questions

Good questions.

Can I start with something small?

Yes, and we’d recommend it. Peach Fix is one tested enquiry path. It’s often the quickest way to see whether we work well together.

Where are you, and who do you work with?

We work internationally, in English, with businesses and brands of all sizes. We focus on the US and UK first.

Will the AI receptionist answer every call?

No. It answers the inbound calls you choose to route to it, within agreed caps, and hands the details to your team. It’s still being tested. We check your number and phone setup before promising anything, and it never makes cold calls.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We agree what “done” looks like before we start and test against it. For Growth Partner work we measure against a baseline we both sign.
