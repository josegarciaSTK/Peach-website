## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners in the US and UK (the service pages name both markets) who run a good business but whose brand, website and enquiry handling have not kept up.

- **First focus:** small, owner-run local businesses, where the owner handles everything and has no marketing or technical person. Examples on the site: a neighbourhood café, a local tour operator, a renovation company.
- **Then:** growing small and medium-sized businesses with a team and some systems in place, where enquiries, brand and follow-up have become messy.
- The owner's job when they arrive: work out whether Peach can fix a specific, nagging problem (enquiries that arrive incomplete or reach nobody, a brand that looks different everywhere, calls they cannot answer) without a big commitment.

Confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-13: start with owner-run businesses through Peach Fix, then move growing businesses on to Peach Partner.

## Product Purpose

Peach is a small studio for brands, websites and the systems behind them. It makes good businesses look as good as they are, then makes every next enquiry easier to win.

Success for a client: the problem named at the start is fixed, tested and handed over, and the business is easier to run and easier to choose.

Success for the website: an owner understands what Peach does in one visit, finds the offer that fits their problem, and gets in touch.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-13. Peach differs from a typical agency or freelancer in three ways:

1. **Design and systems together.** Peach makes the business look good and also fixes how enquiries, bookings and follow-up actually work.
2. **Small, fixed-scope projects.** One problem, a written scope and a fixed price agreed before work starts, tested before handover. No retainer required.
3. **AI and automation where it saves the owner time**, for example Peach Answer, the AI receptionist (in testing).

## Operating Context

The offers, in the order an owner meets them:

- **Peach Fix** (focused project): rebuilds one enquiry path so every enquiry arrives complete and reaches the person responsible, then tests it like a customer would.
- **Brand & Content** (project, with optional care): a clear identity, the files to use it, and an approved content pack; ongoing social on an agreed cadence with the owner's approval before anything is posted.
- **Peach Partner** (growth partner engagement, draft, for businesses that already qualify): starts from a signed baseline with agreed scope, regular reporting and shared responsibilities.
- **Peach Answer** (AI receptionist, draft, in testing): answers the inbound calls the owner routes to it, captures agreed details and hands them to the team.
- **Peach Care**: ongoing care after a project, only when there is a reason for it.

Full offer content lives in `services-data.js` and `service.html`. The process the site describes: find the friction (a short call and a written scope), make it work (design and a tested solution), keep it growing (handover, optional care).

Contact today is by email, reach@peachhq.co. The homepage form prepares an email draft; it does not submit anywhere.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with the owner's problem.** Every offer starts from a concrete, recognisable problem in the owner's words, not from a service category.
2. **Small and certain beats big and vague.** Offer a first step that is fixed in scope, price and outcome before anything larger.
3. **Looks and workings together.** Never present design without what it does for enquiries and follow-up, and never present a system without care for how it looks.
4. **Only real proof.** Show permitted case studies and verified capabilities; label drafts as drafts.
5. **Let the AI earn its place.** Automation is mentioned only where it saves the owner time and only once it has passed its own test.
